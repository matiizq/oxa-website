// Material

Recycled
plastic textile

Each OXA bag is woven by hand using strips of recycled plastic, crafted specifically from fabrics sourced at the markets of Oaxaca.

The fabrics are broken down into weavable strips, then braided using a traditional regional technique. The result is a material that is waterproof, durable, and completely unique.

No two bags share the same pattern. The color of your bag depends on what the market had to offer the day it was made.

"Each square of mesh, woven by hand. That's how every OXA is born."
— OXA · Oaxaca, México
